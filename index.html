🔍 Hex Checker

?How does it work?

  1. Pixels: Every picture is made of tiny dots called pixels. Each one has a hex code like #FF0000. The 3 pairs are how much Red, Green, and Blue light it has (00 = none, FF = the most).
  2. Closest color: We measure the "distance" between your pixel and each of our 12 color buckets, like measuring how far apart two points are on a map. The shortest distance wins.
  3. Sheet music: The picture is read left to right in 64 slices. That makes 4 bars of music (16 beats each). Colors higher up play higher notes.
  4. Sounding good: All notes stay in one key (a family of notes that fit together), drums snap to a steady beat, and the chords change every bar. Bright pictures get a happy major key, dark pictures get a moody minor key, and colorful pictures play faster!
