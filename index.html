🔍 Hex Checker

?How does it work?

  1. Pixels: Every picture is made of tiny dots called pixels. Each one has a hex code like #FF0000. The 3 pairs are how much Red, Green, and Blue light it has (00 = none, FF = the most).
  2. Closest color: We measure the "distance" between your pixel and each of our 12 color buckets, like measuring how far apart two points are on a map. The shortest distance wins.
  3. Sheet music: The picture is read left to right in thin slices, 16 per bar of music. The Song Size slider picks 2, 4, 8, or 16 bars, so bigger songs use more, thinner slices. Colors higher up play higher notes. Upload a GIF and each bar uses a different frame, like a flip book turned into music!
  4. Sounding good: All notes stay in one key (a family of notes that fit together), drums snap to a steady beat, and the chords change every bar. Bright pictures get a happy major key, dark pictures get a moody minor key, and colorful pictures play faster!
  5. Genres: Each genre has its own rules. Pop is bouncy, Rock has crunchy guitars, Hip-Hop swings with a deep 808 bass, EDM thumps on every beat, and Jazz uses fancy 4-note chords and a walking bass. Hot Garbage turns all the rules off, so you hear exactly what your picture says, good or not!
